
# Listening and Active Communication:

## 1. Active Listening:
**1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)**  
Steps/strategies to do Active Listening are:  
1. Avoid getting distracted.
2. Focus on the speaker and the topic.
3. Try not to interrupt the other person, let them finish and then respond.
4. Use door-openers to show that you are interested.
5. Use body language to show your interest.
6. Take notes during important conversations.
7. Paraphrasing the things said by the speaker assures us as active listener.


## 2. Reflective Listening:
**2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)**  
According to Fisher's model, the key points of Reflective Listening are:  
1. Listener should repeat the statements/phrases back to the speaker. This achieves both, assurance of the active participation to the speaker and the clarity of context in the mind of listener, to make sure that both are on the same page.
2. When in a meeting, we must take notes, and later on, share it with all the stakeholders of that meeting. This is essential in meetings having technical discussions, as they are having minute details that cannot be overseen.
3. This ensures the understanding of the needs of the person speaking, which inturn avoids causing issues and errors that may have caused due to carelesness.


## 3. Reflection
**3. What are the obstacles in your listening process?**  
Below are the obstacles in my listening process:  
1. Inward-facing:  
* Short-lived focus
* Lack of interest (due to the topic or speaker)
* Thought of some other priorities/responsibilities/affinities
2. Outward-facing:
* Never reflecting back (reduces speaker's interest)

**4. What can you do to improve your listening?**  
To improve my listening I do the following:  
* Be both, physically as well as mentally present
* One task at a time, with dedicated focus
* Work according to plan and plan according to work
* Keep on interacting as per the necessity during the conversation
    

## 4. Types of Communication
**5. When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?**  
We switch to passive communication at times when we are placing others over ourself. This usually happens when the we are communicating with someone we feel superior to us like family or mentor.


**6. When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?**  
We switch to aggressive communication at times when we are placing ourselves over others. This usually happens when the we are communicating with someone who is subordinate to us or is having less importance.


**7. When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?**  
We switch to passive-aggressive communication at times when we are in a situation where we do not want to follow someone despite being in a sitution where following that person is required. This usually happens when the we are communicating with someone at same level where we hide hostility behind a passive gesture.
