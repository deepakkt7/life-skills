
## Prevention of Sexual Harassment:

**1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?**  
Following listed behaviours can cause sexual harassment:
* Inappropriate gestures like staring, stalking, whistling, etc.
* Inappropriate actions like touching, rubbing against someone, groping, longer handshakes, unwanted hugs, etc.
* Unwelcoming comments or unwanted signals or unacceptable behaviour like showing something explicit.
* Digital sexual harassment like sending obnoxious emails, messages, videos, pictures or sorts.
* Demanding sexual favours for any positive or negetive steps.
* Gender-based treatment.

**2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?**  
In case we face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such sexual harassment behaviour, we should do the following:
1. Dare to say "NO". This should be the very first step in preventing any such acts in the future. It works in approximately 90% of cases.
2. Report to your manager about the incident in case of reoccurrence.
3. If the offender itself is among the senior authorities, we should directly seek help from the organization's responsible authority like the ICC (Internal Complaint Committee).
4. If nothing from above works, and the actions are still repeated, we should go directly to the local police authority.
5. If still facing or witnessing such issues, compaint to national-level grievance redressal mechanisms specifically designed for woman's rights (Sexual Harassment of Women at the Workplace Act, 2013) and human rights.
