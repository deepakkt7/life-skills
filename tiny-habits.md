### 2. Tiny Habits by BJ Fogg - Core Message
**Q2. How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.**  
To make making the new habits easier we should be having very tiniest of action or the smallest part of the task in which we want to do big. This requires least motivation and very little of the effort (ability) that it can be done easily but only after it is following some prompt (trigger).
In the given idea,'B' stands for behaviour, 'M' for motivation, 'A' for the ability, and 'P' for the prompt.

### 4. Book Summary of Atomic Habits
**Q5. What is the book's perspective about Identity?**  
The habits, that we want to inculcate in our life, should be something like it has became a part of our identity.

**Q6. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?**  
As per book's perspective, to make a habit easier to do, we need to have:
* the tiny step "obvious" to do;
* that step look "attractive" as well for our internal motivation;
* that the little step "easy" as well;
* as if taking even this little step is so "satisfying" or rewarding that we are willing to repeat it next time.

**Q7. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?**  
As per book's perspective, to make a habit harder to do, we need to have:
* the "invisibility" of our task subject or trigger;
* that thing even look "fugly" or gives feeling of disgust even by thinking of doing it;
* that little step looks so "hard" even to start;
* the non-rewarding or "unsatisfying" outcome of the step, even if taken, which makes it more and more harder to start next time.
