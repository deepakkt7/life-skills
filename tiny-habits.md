### 2. Tiny Habits by BJ Fogg - Core Message
**Q2. How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.**  
To make making the new habits easier we should be having very tiniest of action or the smallest part of the task in which we want to do big. This requires least motivation and very little of the effort (ability) that it can be done easily but only after it is following some prompt (trigger).
In the given idea,'B' stands for behaviour, 'M' for motivation, 'A' for the ability, and 'P' for the prompt.


**Q3. Why it is important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit? (This is the most important concept in today's topic. Whatever you celebrate becomes a habit)**  
The "Shine" is kind of a reward to ourselves which actually works by increasing our motivation everytime we complete our act of habit successfully. This motivation keeps us guiding towards making the habit.
