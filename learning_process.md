**3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**  
Modes of thinking:  
1. **Active Mode:** This is the state of mind where it is fully focused and concentrated.  
2. **Diffused Mode:** In this mode, the we are usually relaxed, utilizing subconscious mode of thinking. It allows the brain to look into the bug picture (e.g., thoughts while taking shower).

## Learn Anything in 20 hours
**4. According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**  
The steps to take when approaching a new topic are:
1. Decontruct the skill that we want to learn (break it down into its components).
2. Learn enough to question and correct yourself.
3. Remove all the distractions in practice.
4. Practice for at least 20 hours to get good enough on that skill.
